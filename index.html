About the Academy

The TTAO Inner Academy is a structured three-year sanctuary for sincere practitioners of the Hermetic Arts. Rooted in the Paracelsian tradition, the curriculum weaves together practical laboratory alchemy (plant, water, and mineral), inner work, astrology, Qabalah, and the living philosophy of the Great Work.

Year One — The Green Lion — is offered entirely free as a complete foundational course, allowing genuine students to prove their commitment through direct experience before deeper investment.

Beyond the classical curriculum, the Academy maintains a unique living system known as Glyphic Cybermancy: a custom Discord and Stoat bot that serves as both a practical tool and a technological extension of the Temple’s symbolic language. The bot offers glyph decoding, mythic interpretation, resonance indexing, sigil generation, and other specialized functions. Higher-tier commands are gated behind Patreon authentication, ensuring the sustainability of API services, domain hosting, and self-hosted infrastructure.

This integration of ancient alchemical principles with modern technology reflects the Order’s ethos: to preserve and transmit the Art through whatever vessels best serve the Work in this age.
