First Step

The Silent Vigil

Before touching any laboratory equipment, a student must produce the STONE within first. This begins with a consistent vigil of nature: at least 10 minutes a day, at the same time and spot, for one year. Use this time for analytical contemplation of natural phenomena — the foundation upon which all further Work is built.
