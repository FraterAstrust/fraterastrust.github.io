How the Academy Functions

The TTAO Inner Academy operates as a competency-based progression through the vegetable, water, and mineral kingdoms of alchemy.

Each year contains:

  • Monthly laboratory modules
  • Parallel inner work practices
  • Quarterly Gatherings
  • Laboratory and journal documentation
  • Practical operative milestones

Students progress through demonstrated attainment rather than time enrolled.

The Green Lion curriculum culminates in the formation of a Procedurally Complete Plant Stone, which serves as the foundational competency threshold for advancement into Year Two.

About the Academy

The TTAO Inner Academy offers a structured environment for practitioners of the Hermetic Arts seeking disciplined study rather than fragmented information.

The curriculum develops practical spagyric laboratory work, inner discipline, planetary timing, and Hermetic philosophy in parallel — following the classical Paracelsian model of integrated development.

Students maintain journals, laboratory records, and participate in Quarterly Gatherings to document progression through the Work.

The Academy infrastructure includes a custom Discord and Stoat bot system built around Glyphic Cybermancy, providing symbolic indexing, planetary utilities, glyph interpretation, and curriculum integration tools.

All primary lesson materials are delivered directly through email and the Academy Dashboard to support uninterrupted long-term study.

⚠️ THE LAW OF PROGRESSION

Advancement within the TTAO Inner Academy is competency-based — never financial.

Patreon support grants access to lessons, community systems, research materials, and Academy infrastructure. It does not grant advancement into Year Two or Year Three.

Entry into the Philosophic Waters and Grey Wolf curriculum requires direct verification of operative attainment, beginning with the successful formation of a Procedurally Complete Plant Stone.

The Work advances through demonstrated understanding, disciplined practice, and verified laboratory results.

“The theory is dry without the praxis. Doing the laboratory work will thoroughly ingrain the theory and make it LIVE within and without you.”
