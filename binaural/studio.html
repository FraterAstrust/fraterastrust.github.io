Binaural Studio

Facilitate · Do Not Simulate

A tuning aid for meditation. It helps you settle into and hold the frame of mind in which an experience can unfold on its own. It does not simulate an experience, recreate imagery from one, or promise a result. Either the experience happens or it does not.

Before you begin

Read this once, then close your eyes and listen.

Locus of awareness

Keep your awareness at the third-eye area, in the centre of the head, as a locus. This is a locus, not a posture rule. Eyes closed throughout.

  • Headphones required. Binaural tones only work with separate sound in each ear. Do not use speakers.
  • No spoken content. There is no guidance voice, no subliminal, and no affirmation anywhere in this studio.
  • Gentle volume. Start low. Do not listen while driving, or if you are prone to seizures.
  • No return. There is no timed ending and no return phase. Natural return comes first, then you stop the audio when you are back to waking consciousness — like waking to turn off an alarm. The only exception is the safety stop at 2½ hours.
